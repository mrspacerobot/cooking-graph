## What did not fit

- The sentence rule that refuses "runs clear" as a claim about cooked meat also refused it for rinsing water. The cue was reworded.
- Toasting bread, toasting nuts and toasting spices are one card. Whether dry-roasting a spice is the same move as toasting a slice of bread is open.
- A line of a recipe names the form an ingredient is in, such as peeled, and no step says who peeled it. That was already so in cycle one.
