# Filling the gaps the swap questions named

Everything here is tier `drafted`. The words came from `./probe questions`, which lists only growing gaps. Two drafters worked apart: one on ingredients, one on dishes.

## What came in

- 17 ingredients: Greek yogurt, Japanese short-grain rice, Pecorino Romano, arborio rice, bomba rice, cheddar, cottage cheese, chicken breast, coconut milk, dried apricot, nora pepper, peanut butter, quinoa, skimmed milk, white miso, red miso and white vinegar. All 21 safety facts are answered, strict where unsure. All safety records stay unverified.
- 6 aliases that name exactly the thing (pine nuts, pistachios, chickpeas, dried chickpeas, fresh mozzarella, fine dry breadcrumbs). "Dried chickpeas" is the weakest, because it is a form word.
- 5 dishes with recipes: bouillabaisse, izgara kofte, mercimek corbasi, naan and pommes puree. One new preparation (naan dough) and 3 technique cards, each a variant of its nearest cousin with what differs.
- The graph is now 446 ingredients, 161 dishes and 179 recipes.

## Left out on purpose

- Forms of an ingredient that is already there (cracked black pepper, fresh lemon, canned chickpeas), and lardons, which is already a form.
- Two dishes a growing question names, because the checker said a held-back probe asks about each. They were deleted and not worked around, and they are not written here.
- Nigella seed and orange zest were missing for naan and bouillabaisse. The nearest existing ingredient was used.

## What it did not do

The blind swap questions still answer 1 of 32 growing. Adding ingredients and recipes was not enough, for reasons that are plain in the gap list:
- Many questions name a dish with no recipe (pizza margherita, baklava, creme brulee, chicken tikka masala, tzatziki, pesto, risotto).
- Some name a recipe that exists but has no line for the ingredient asked about, for example a rice dish that lists plain rice and not short-grain rice.
- Some need a swap row and a recipe line at once, and rows exist only for pairs the drafters judged real.
So answering a swap question depends on the exact recipe, which makes it a fragile measure. Treat the count as a gap list, not a score.

## Doubts

- Greek yogurt is a separate ingredient under strained yogurt. It could be argued as an alias.
- Cheddar is in the grating cheeses group, and a card there says "melts smoothly", which aged cheddar may not.
- Bouillabaisse and the existing fish soup share most of their broth. The difference is a strained broth and fish served in order.
- Two new technique words (make naan dough, cook a naan) are Indian words that other cuisines may also draft.
