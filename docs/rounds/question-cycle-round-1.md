## The model and the tools

An exclusion-only statement needs `allows: false`; otherwise an empty `when`
would claim it works everywhere outside the forbidden context. Existing rows
are explicitly positive. The checker enforces negative contexts and rejects
positive contexts on exclusion-only rows. `docs/model.md` logs the example.

For a name-only stand-in probe, a unique variation of the same dish may supply
its original if the named recipe does not use it. An explicit recipe id stays
binding. Several matching variations fail as ambiguous. This is probe matching,
not step adaptation in the question commands.

No old probe, safety fact or safety disagreement was edited. The strict-answer
document was regenerated by `./safety strict` after adding ingredients.

## The reread and independent check

Ten new files were reread: the six ingredient files, poached eggs as dish and
recipe, and the two sauce variations. The copied mayonnaise method incorrectly
said vinegar after its ingredient changed to lemon juice. It was corrected.

`gpt-6-sol` then reviewed the tools and graph changes without opening `probes/`.
It found an older olive-oil statement that wrongly offered oil for the cold
butter mounting the chicken pan sauce. This was reproduced through the real
`./stand-ins` command, given a failing test, and corrected with an explicit
prohibition. Its dead positive context was removed.

The review also found that an explicit probe recipe id could select another
variation; the failing test now proves it stays binding. A new parsley-stems
alias and an older ground-black-pepper alias were removed: stems cannot fill
a leafy garnish and ground pepper cannot be crushed into coarse peppercorns.
The command does not infer that unsuitable forms can be reversed.

The reviewer doubted required vinegar in poached eggs, which is now optional,
and the courgette-heavy ratatouille option. The latter remains a drafted
variation with its difference stated, pending a cuisine reviewer.
