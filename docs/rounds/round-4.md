## Changes to the model: none

## The reread

Ten of the new files were read again. Two were wrong and were fixed:

- The hachis parmentier told the cook to check "the thickest part" of minced beef.
- The tartiflette softened its onions with the sweat technique "until golden" and deglazed a fond that the sweating was said to leave. The fond is left by the bacon. The onions are only softened.

## What the model could not express

- **Two heats in one step.** A leg of lamb starts in a hot oven and finishes in a gentler one. A step has one temperature. The recipe gives one.
- **Either the stove or the oven.** A braise can be held at a simmer on the stove or in a low oven. A step cannot offer both.
- **A step that runs while another runs.** The chicken is basted during its roasting. The steps are a list, so basting comes "after" roasting and carries no time.
- **A form no technique makes.** The duck confit of the parmentier is "shredded". No technique in the graph shreds.
- **What a dish is served with.** Blanquette wants rice, a braise wants potatoes or noodles. A dish has parts, and no link to another dish at its side.
- Still open from earlier rounds: the French sauter, doneness, waiting time, proportions, a leftover, keeping a sauce warm, a need that holds only for some food, a part that may be left out, the numbers that define a technique, one thing a cook either buys or makes, and the empty `forms` of every ingredient.
