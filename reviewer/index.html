A seat at the kitchen table

A little of your know-how
goes a long way.

Read a lesson, a recipe or an ingredient swap, the way you would read a recipe card. Then tell us what is clear and what you would do differently.

  1. Pick something from the list. Recipes are a good place to start.
  2. Read it slowly, as if you were about to cook it.
  3. Give it a score, write a note, or both. Then send it.
